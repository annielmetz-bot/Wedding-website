How it happened

Our Story

Annie and Marcus met during a brief but chaotic shared living situation in Washington, D.C., in 2018. They became fast friends while navigating the quirks of their group house, and somewhere between late-night kitchen conversations and impromptu adventures, something shifted.

They've since traded D.C. for the road — from the red rocks of New Mexico to ferry rides in Scandinavia, from Oregon lakeshores to Caribbean beaches. Every trip has been proof that the best adventures are better together.

Now they're inviting their favorite people to share the next big one. A weekend in the mountains of northern New Mexico, surrounded by people they love, in a place that feels like magic.

Welcome Party

Where: Juliette at Hotel Willa, Taos

Kick off the weekend with us at Juliette, the beautiful bar and restaurant inside Hotel Willa in downtown Taos. Come meet the wedding party, grab a drink, and get in the mood for a New Mexico weekend. Casual and low-key — stop in whenever works for you.

The Wedding

Where: 48 Rim Road, Arroyo Seco, NM

The ceremony and reception will take place at a private property in Arroyo Seco with sweeping views of the Sangre de Cristo Mountains. More details — including timing, dress code tips, and what to expect — are in the FAQ.
