Our Story

We met later in life, when we knew who we were and what we wanted. We discovered in one another a shared passion for lives lived intentionally, if also unconventionally.

The time it took to find each other allowed us to build deep relationships with ourselves, with the people around us, and with the broader world. One of the gifts of marrying later in life is the richness of the community we get to gather.

This place, these mountains, this way of living: they've become the foundation of our happiness. We spend our days together, making things, learning, exploring, enjoying the seasons, and building a life grounded in this particular plot of earth. Having you witness our marriage in this place we cherish is a joy we can't quite put into words. We hope you can make it.
