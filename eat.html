New Mexican food is its own thing

Places to Eat

The answer to "red or green?" is always "Christmas." You'll know what that means soon enough.

Red or Green?

New Mexican cuisine revolves around chile — red and green. When they ask "red or green?" at a restaurant, "Christmas" means you want both. Green chile tends to be fresher and earthier; red is richer and smokier. Opinions are strong. You'll develop your own.

Taos — Breakfast & Lunch

Breakfast

Michael's Kitchen

Downtown Taos · Cash-friendly · Opens early

A Taos institution since 1974. Big, casual, always busy. Known for massive huevos rancheros, breakfast burritos, and green chile everything. The kind of place where locals and tourists happily share booths. Get there early or expect a wait.

Breakfast / Lunch

Gutiz

North Taos · French–NM fusion

A charming café that blends French technique with New Mexican ingredients. Think green chile croissants, huevos with a Gallic twist, and excellent pastries. A little out of the way (north of downtown) but absolutely worth it. Beloved by locals.

Coffee & Pastries

Wired? Coffee Bar

Downtown Taos

Our go-to for good espresso in downtown Taos. Cozy, friendly, good pastries. The kind of place you settle in with a book while the morning traffic goes by on the plaza.

Lunch

Orlando's New Mexican Café

El Prado · North of downtown

Consistently ranked among the best New Mexican food in the area. Famous for their carne adovada, enchiladas, and fresh tortillas. Small, colorful, and always packed. No reservations — show up early or be prepared to wait (it's worth it).

Breakfast / Lunch

Taos Diner

Downtown Taos

Exactly what the name suggests — a classic diner with great New Mexican breakfast. Go for the breakfast burrito smothered in green chile and don't look back. Unpretentious and delicious.

Lunch

Love Apple

North Taos · Farm-to-table

Set in a tiny historic adobe chapel, the Love Apple serves beautifully executed farm-to-table New Mexican food. The atmosphere alone is worth the visit — candles, exposed adobe, intimate. Dinner only on most nights; check for lunch hours.

🌙

Taos — Dinner

Dinner · Nice

Sabroso

El Prado · sabrosorestaurant.com

One of the best restaurants in the Taos area — elegant but unpretentious, with a strong focus on local ingredients and a great wine list. Beautiful setting with mountain views. Make reservations in advance. A splurge worth making.

Dinner · Casual

Antonio's

Downtown Taos

Excellent New Mexican food in a beautiful old adobe downtown. Great mole, killer green chile, and a warm, festive atmosphere. One of those restaurants that feels exactly right for the place. Reservations recommended on weekends.

Dinner · Bar

Juliette at Hotel Willa

Downtown Taos (Friday welcome party!)

The bar and restaurant at Hotel Willa — this is where we're having the Friday welcome party! Great cocktails, beautiful space, excellent small plates. Even if you're not coming Friday, worth a visit for drinks or dinner.

Dinner

The Gorge Bar & Grill

Downtown Taos

Right on the plaza with a great patio. Good burgers, green chile everything, and solid margaritas. More casual and lively than some of the other options — good for a bigger group or when you want a fun, no-fuss dinner.

Dinner

El Gamal

Downtown Taos · Middle Eastern

A tiny, warmly lit spot serving excellent Middle Eastern food — falafel, hummus, lamb, and vegetarian options that stand out in a region dominated by New Mexican cuisine. A nice change of pace if you want something different.

Dinner · Pizza

The Burger Stand

Downtown Taos

Great green chile cheeseburgers, local craft beers, and a lively bar scene. One of those spots that's hard to leave once you've settled in. Good for a casual night out.

Arroyo Seco & El Prado

The area just north of Taos, near the wedding venue, has a few gems worth knowing about.

Ice Cream · Must Do

Taos Cow

Arroyo Seco village

The best ice cream in New Mexico, made with local milk and featuring flavors like Taos Cow Crunch, Chile Caramel, and Café Olé. Right in the heart of Arroyo Seco village. Get a cone. Get two. We're not judging.

Breakfast / Lunch

Abe's Cantina y Cocina

Arroyo Seco village

A tiny, classic New Mexican spot right in the village. Basic menu, great green chile, cold beer. The kind of place where the food is honest and the vibe is completely unpretentious. Cash only. Perfect pre- or post-hike stop.

Breakfast / Coffee

Elevation Coffee

El Prado · Near the venue

A neighborhood coffee shop with good espresso and a relaxed local vibe. Great if you're staying in the Arroyo Seco/El Prado area and want to avoid the drive into town for your morning coffee.

⛰️

Taos Ski Valley

About 20 minutes up the mountain from town — worth the drive for a meal with a view.

Dinner · Special Occasion

The Bavarian

Taos Ski Valley · thebavarian.net

A legendary mountain restaurant at the base of the ski valley — schnitzel, spaetzle, fondue, and cold German beer in a beautiful wood-beamed lodge. Feels completely incongruous and completely perfect. A Taos classic. Make reservations.

Drinks & Views

The Black Diamond Rendezvous

Taos Ski Valley

Great après-ski-style bar in the ski valley — even in September, the mountain views from the deck are spectacular. Good cocktails, hearty mountain food, and a lively crowd. Great for a drink after hiking Williams Lake.
