Where to Stay

Book early!

Taos is a popular destination and the area fills up quickly in September. We recommend booking as soon as possible. There's no room block, so just book directly with the hotels.

Hotel Willa

Downtown Taos · hotelwilla.com

Our first choice and the spot for the Friday welcome party. Hotel Willa is a beautifully restored boutique hotel right on the Taos Plaza with a great bar (Juliette) and restaurant downstairs. Walkable to almost everything in downtown Taos.

Vibe: Boutique, stylish, right in the heart of town.

El Monte Sagrado

Taos · elmontesagrado.com

A beautiful resort set on the Rio Pueblo just steps from the Taos Plaza. Gorgeous grounds, a great spa, and the kind of lush, dramatic setting you want for a New Mexico trip. A step up in luxury if you're looking to treat yourself.

Vibe: Lush resort, spa, upscale.

Hacienda del Sol

North Taos · haciendadelsoltaos.com

A historic adobe inn that once sheltered D.H. Lawrence and his wife Frieda. Beautiful gardens, stunning mountain views, and the kind of quiet, artsy ambiance that makes Taos special. About 10 minutes north of the Plaza, very close to the wedding venue.

Vibe: Historic, intimate, peaceful. Great mountain views.

Palacio de Marquesa

Downtown Taos · marquesataos.com

A charming collection of casitas and suites surrounding a central courtyard in downtown Taos. Perfect for groups or anyone who wants a little more privacy and space. Beautifully decorated with local art and traditional New Mexican style.

Vibe: Casita-style, cozy, local art throughout.

Sagebrush Inn

South Taos · sagebrushinn.com

A Taos classic that's been hosting artists and travelers since 1929. Georgia O'Keeffe used to stay here. Less expensive than some of the other options, with a great Southwestern character and a lively bar scene. A few miles south of the Plaza.

Vibe: Classic, unpretentious, good value. Old Taos energy.

Vacation Rentals

Arroyo Seco, El Prado & Taos · Airbnb / VRBO

The Arroyo Seco and El Prado area (close to the venue) has lots of beautiful vacation rentals — adobe casitas, artist compounds, houses with mountain views. Great option if you're coming as a group or want more space. Search on Airbnb or VRBO.

Vibe: Varies widely. Can be magical. Book early!

Getting Here

Taos does not have a commercial airport, so you'll need to fly into one of the nearby cities and rent a car. We strongly recommend renting a car — you'll need it to get around once you're there.

Albuquerque (ABQ)

~2.5 hours south · Best option for most travelers

The easiest and most affordable airport for most people. Albuquerque Sunport (ABQ) has flights from most major hubs. From ABQ, drive north on I-25 to US-285 to NM-68 — a beautiful drive through the Rio Grande Gorge. About 2.5 hours.

Bonus: the drive up through the gorge is stunning. Worth the trip in itself.

Santa Fe (SAF)

~1.5 hours south · Small regional airport

Santa Fe has a small regional airport with limited flights from certain cities (Dallas, Denver, Los Angeles, Phoenix). If it's convenient, it cuts the drive significantly — about 1.5 hours to Taos. Car rental options are more limited, so book early.

Denver (DEN)

~5 hours north · Worth it if you love a road trip

Denver is a longer drive (~5 hours through the mountains via US-160 and NM-522, or ~5.5 via I-25 and US-285), but if you're coming from the Midwest or want to make a bigger trip out of it, it's a beautiful drive. Especially scenic in September.

Getting Around

You need a car

Taos is a small town, but the region is spread out — the Plaza, Arroyo Seco, El Prado, and Taos Ski Valley are all a few miles apart. Rent a car. Rideshare options are limited and unreliable in this area.

Tips

Parking

Parking is free and easy throughout Taos. Downtown has a large free lot behind the Plaza, and most hotels and restaurants have their own parking. At the wedding venue, we'll have more details closer to the date.

Tips

From Venue to Town

The wedding venue (48 Rim Road, Arroyo Seco) is about 10 minutes north of the Taos Plaza and about 5 minutes from the Arroyo Seco village. You'll want a car or a designated driver — or plan to stay somewhere nearby.

Tips

Altitude

Taos sits at about 7,000 feet (Arroyo Seco is closer to 7,500). If you're coming from sea level, give yourself a day to adjust before the wedding. Drink extra water and go easy on alcohol the first day. See our FAQ for more tips.
